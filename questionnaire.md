# Guitar Lead Funnel — Questionnaire UX & Result Design

## Overall Journey

> **Meta Ad ➔ Assessment ➔ Personalized result ➔ FREE 15-minute virtual consultation OR free guitar resources**

The assessment should feel like a useful mini-product, not a sales form.

There are three separate entry experiences, but all feed into the same Python backend and Google Sheets CRM.

### Opening
**Which Songs Could YOU Play on Guitar? 🎸**

### Questions

| # | Question | Options |

| **Q1** | Do you currently own a guitar? | • Yes, I have one 🎸<br>• Not yet, but I'm planning to get one<br>• No |
| **Q2** | What can you currently play? | • Nothing yet<br>• A few chords<br>• Chords + basic strumming<br>• I can already play complete songs |
| **Q3** | What kind of music would you most like to play? | • Bollywood / Hindi songs<br>• Pop / acoustic<br>• Rock / indie<br>• Fingerstyle / instrumental<br>• Something else |
| **Q4** | How many songs would you eventually love to have in your repertoire? | • Just 1–2 favourites<br>• 3–5 songs<br>• 10+ songs<br>• I want to be able to pick up a guitar and play almost anything |
| **Q5** | How much time could you realistically practice? | • <10 min/day<br>• 10–20 min<br>• 20–30 min<br>• 30+ min |
| **Q6** | How soon would you like to start playing your first song? | • No rush<br>• Within a few months<br>• Within a month<br>• As soon as possible |

### Result Direction
This result should be highly concrete and music-specific.
The system should combine:
1. Music preference
2. Current ability
3. Optionally practice commitment

It should then recommend a small set of songs or song categories.

> [!NOTE]
> **Example: Indie + knows a few chords**
> You already have enough foundation to move beyond isolated chord practice. Your next step is working on chord transitions and consistent rhythm.
> **Suggested songs:**
> - Barishein — beginner/intermediate depending on version
> - CO2 — suitable if the user's chord/rhythm level matches
> - 1–2 slightly more challenging songs as a stretch goal

> [!NOTE]
> **Example: Indie + can already play complete songs**
> You're ready to move from basic song playing toward cleaner transitions, more expressive rhythm and more demanding arrangements.

**The result should include:**
- Their current approximate level
- Why the recommended songs fit
- 3–5 recommended songs
- 1–2 "next challenge" songs
- What skill they should develop next
- A short progression toward their preferred music

*The exact song list should come from predefined mappings rather than an LLM.*

**Example mapping concept:**
- **Music preference:** Indie, Bollywood, Pop, Rock, Fingerstyle
- **Ability:** Beginner / no playing, Basic chords, Chords + strumming, Complete songs

## AD 3 — How Long Until YOU Can Play Guitar?

### Opening
**How Long Would It Take YOU to Play Your First Guitar Song? 🎸**
Answer 6 quick questions and we'll estimate your learning path based on your current experience, practice time and goal.

### Questions

| # | Question | Options |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Q1** | First things first — do you currently own a guitar? | • Yes 🎸<br>• Not yet, but I'm planning to buy one<br>• No, I'm just exploring |
| **Q2** | What's your current level? | • I've never played<br>• I've tried a few times<br>• I know some basic chords<br>• I can already play songs |
| **Q3** | How much time can you realistically practice? | • Less than 10 min/day<br>• 10–20 min/day<br>• 20–30 min/day<br>• 30+ min/day |
| **Q4** | What's your first big guitar goal? | • Just learn the basics<br>• Play my favourite songs<br>• Sing while playing<br>• Play confidently for others<br>• Become an advanced guitarist |
| **Q5** | How consistently could you practice? | • Whenever I get time<br>• 1–2 days/week<br>• 3–4 days/week<br>• Almost every day |
| **Q6** | When would you ideally like to start? | • I'm just curious<br>• Maybe someday<br>• Within the next few months<br>• Within the next few weeks |

### Result Direction
The result should not simply say "4–8 weeks." It should connect the estimate to the user's answers and give a realistic path.

> [!NOTE]
> **Example: Current level: Basic Chord Player**
> If: owns guitar + knows basic chords + practices 20–30 mins/day + practices 3–4+ days/week
> 
> **You're closer than you might think.**
> With your current foundation and 20–30 minutes of consistent practice, you're already past the hardest "where do I start?" stage.
> Your next milestone should be getting your chord transitions smooth enough to maintain rhythm while playing.
> **Songs you could aim for next:**
> - Song A — first milestone
> - Song B — slightly harder
> - Song C — stretch goal
> 
> If you maintain your practice routine, a realistic target could be completing your first few songs within roughly X–Y weeks.

> [!NOTE]
> **Another combination:**
> If: owns guitar + complete beginner + 10 mins/day + inconsistent practice
> 
> **Your first milestone is not learning a full song yet — it's building the foundation.**
> Start with 3–4 basic chords, simple rhythm and short daily practice.
> Once those become comfortable, you can move into your first simple song.
> **Your likely progression:**
> 1. Basic chord shapes
> 2. Chord transitions
> 3. Simple rhythm
> 4. First complete song
> 
> With consistent practice, your timeline will depend heavily on increasing practice frequency.

**Possible result variables:**
- Current ability
- Practice commitment
- Guitar ownership
- Goal

**The output should contain:**
- Current starting point
- Estimated milestone range
- What they should learn next
- 2–3 appropriate song examples
- What would accelerate their progress
- What to expect over the next few weeks

> [!WARNING]
> The estimate must always be presented as an approximate range, not a guarantee.

## Result Engine — Product/UX Principle

> [!IMPORTANT]
> The result should feel like a **mini personalized report**, not a score.

A strong result should answer:
1. Where am I now?
2. What does that mean?
3. What should I learn next?
4. What could I realistically play?
5. What is my next milestone?
6. How can an expert help me?

### Personalization Strategy
> [!CAUTION]
> Do NOT build a unique result for every possible combination. Instead use a small rules/mapping system.

**Example for Assessment 2:**
`music_style + ability_level -> recommendation_group`
- Indie + basic chords -> Indie Beginner/Chord Group
- Indie + complete songs -> Indie Intermediate Group
- Bollywood + basic chords -> Bollywood Beginner/Chord Group
- Bollywood + complete songs -> Bollywood Intermediate Group

*Then optionally adjust the wording using practice commitment.*

**Example for Assessment 3:**
`current_level + practice_commitment -> timeline/result group`
- Beginner + low practice -> Foundation Group
- Beginner + high practice -> Fast Foundation Group
- Basic chords + low practice -> Transition Group
- Basic chords + high practice -> First Songs Group
- Complete songs + high practice -> Next Level Group

*This provides substantial personalization without requiring an LLM.*

## Important UX Principle

> [!WARNING]
> The final CTA should not abruptly sell the course.

The journey is:
1. Ad
2. Curiosity
3. Assessment
4. Personalized result
5. Useful recommendations
6. "Want expert help?"
7. **FREE 15-minute consultation**
8. Counsellor
9. Course

The consultation is positioned as the next logical step, not as a sales pitch.
