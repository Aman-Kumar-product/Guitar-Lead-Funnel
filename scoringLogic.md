# Guitar Lead Funnel - Scoring Logic

---

## Assessment 1: Learning Profile Scoring

| # | Question | Options & Points |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | Do you currently own a guitar? | • Yes (25 pts)<br>• No, but planning to buy one soon (15 pts)<br>• No (0 pts) |
| **2** | What's your current experience? | • Complete beginner (5 pts)<br>• Tried learning before (10 pts)<br>• Know basic chords (15 pts)<br>• Can already play songs (15 pts) |
| **3** | What would make you happiest after learning guitar? | • Understand the basics (5 pts)<br>• Play a few songs (10 pts)<br>• Sing and play (15 pts)<br>• Perform confidently (15 pts) |
| **4** | How do you prefer learning? | • Explore by myself (5 pts)<br>• YouTube/tutorials (8 pts)<br>• Structured course (12 pts)<br>• Teacher guiding me (15 pts) |
| **5** | How much can you realistically practice? | • <10 min/day (5 pts)<br>• 10–20 min (10 pts)<br>• 20–30 min (12 pts)<br>• 30+ min (15 pts) |
| **6** | When would you ideally start? | • Just exploring (3 pts)<br>• In a few months (7 pts)<br>• Within a month (12 pts)<br>• Within 2 weeks (15 pts) |
